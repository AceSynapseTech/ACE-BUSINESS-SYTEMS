  1. Pay via Lipa na M-Pesa → PayBill 714888, account 480457.
  2. Copy the complete M-Pesa confirmation message.
  3. Paste it into ACE (Settings → Subscription & Billing).
  4. ACE extracts the transaction code, amount, status, recipient, account, date, time and fee, then validates them. Verified amounts count towards your subscription.

Pay in instalments. You do not have to pay KSh 1,500 at once, for example KSh 600 + KSh 500 + KSh 400. Each M-Pesa message is submitted separately, and only verified payments count. Extra money is kept as account credit.

Example: KSh 600 + KSh 500 + KSh 400 = KSh 1,500

ACE reads and validates your message. Payments may need final verification before being applied; ACE does not claim instant confirmation with Safaricom.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free trial?

Yes. New customers receive 30 days free.

How much does ACE cost?

The standard monthly subscription is KSh 1,500. A discounted yearly option is also available.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. Make several M-Pesa payments toward the subscription target. Verified payments are added together.

How do I pay?

M-Pesa PayBill 714888, account 480457.

What do I submit after paying?

Paste the complete M-Pesa confirmation message into the payment form.

Is payment automatically confirmed?

ACE automatically reads and validates your message. Payments are then confirmed through the ACE verification process; we do not claim instant confirmation with Safaricom.

What if I pay more than required?

The excess is kept as account credit according to the subscription rules.

What happens when my subscription expires?

Your business data remains preserved. Renew from the subscription page.

Can I pay yearly?

Yes, ACE offers a discounted yearly option.

How does the referral program work?

Refer a new business with your code or link. When it meets the qualifying conditions, you earn KSh 500.

Help Center

How do I get started?

Select Get Started, create your account, choose a template, then add your items and customers.

Can I use my own logo and colours?

Yes. Upload a PNG, JPG or WebP logo and pick a brand colour when you set up, or later in Settings.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. The layout adapts to phones, tablets and computers.

How do I record an M-Pesa payment?

Choose M-Pesa as the payment method when completing a sale. Automatic confirmation is not connected yet.

Where is my data stored?

Securely in the cloud, so you can sign in from any device and see the same records.
